About Us

Founded in 2025, Bookr was created with a simple vision: to make discovering and enjoying books easier and more inspiring for everyone. From timeless classics to the latest releases, Bookr offers a wide collection that caters to every kind of reader. Beyond being just a bookstore, we aim to be a community—an inviting space where stories connect people, ideas spark conversations, and every page turned feels like home.

More Than Books

Bookr goes beyond the shelves, offering carefully curated merchandise that complements every reader’s lifestyle. From premium stationery and creative art supplies to unique notebooks, vinyl records, and collectible finds, we bring together items that inspire imagination and reflect the individuality of our community.
